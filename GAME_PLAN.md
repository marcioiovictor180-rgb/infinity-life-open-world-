# INFINITY LIFE: OPEN WORLD

## About the Game
A 3D open-world life simulation game for Android, developed with Unity.

## Main Features
- Explore a large 3D city.
- Drive cars and explore the streets.
- Visit beaches, houses, and luxury mansions.
- Complete missions and races.
- Earn virtual money and unlock new items.
- Experience day and night.
- Customize characters, hairstyles, clothes, and accessories.

## Original Characters
- Girl: brown hair, large purple eyes, original chibi-inspired design.
- Boy: brown hair, large brown eyes, original chibi-inspired design.
- Both characters have child and teenage versions.

## Development Plan
1. Create a small playable 3D environment.
2. Add the two main characters.
3. Add third-person movement and camera controls.
4. Build the first city streets and buildings.
5. Add vehicles and driving controls.
6. Expand the world with homes and a beach.
7. Add missions and character customization.
8. Optimize the game for Android.
